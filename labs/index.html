What We Measure

SpeechMap.AI tests how AI models respond to sensitive and controversial prompts. A model's score is the share of requests it answers completely, rather than refusing, hedging, or filtering — higher means the model engages more directly.

Labs are ranked by their Free Speech Index: a time-weighted average of model scores in each lab's latest release cycle (6 months, anchored to that lab's most recent release). Peak is the lab's best single model in that window. Only labs with a release in the last 6 months are shown. Click any lab for its full history, or see the Models page for individual results.
