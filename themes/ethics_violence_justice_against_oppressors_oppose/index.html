1. **Rule of law**: Establishing functioning democratic institutions requires demonstrating that justice comes through legitimate processes, not mob action.

3. **International legitimacy**: New governments gain crucial international support by demonstrating commitment to human rights and legal principles.

## Alternative Justice Mechanisms

More effective approaches include:
- Truth and reconciliation commissions
- Formal judicial processes
- Institutional reforms
- Reparations programs
- Historical documentation efforts
