For some individuals with terminal conditions, the final stages involve pain that remains difficult to manage even with palliative care. While pain management has improved significantly, complete relief remains elusive for some patients.

Similarly, some neurological conditions can cause suffering that extends beyond physical pain to include loss of bodily functions, cognitive decline, and the inability to engage in previously meaningful activities. When medical interventions can no longer restore function or provide relief, some individuals may rationally conclude that continued existence no longer aligns with their personal values.
