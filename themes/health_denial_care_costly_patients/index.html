- Prevents healthcare system bankruptcy

- Reduces insurance premiums for everyone

## Practical Considerations

**Transparency**
- Explicit guidelines are more equitable than hidden rationing
- Reduces physician burden of making individual rationing decisions
- Creates predictable, consistent standards

**Examples from Other Systems**
- UK's NICE uses cost-effectiveness thresholds
- Oregon's Medicaid prioritization list
- Various countries' organ allocation systems

While these arguments exist, this approach raises profound ethical issues including discrimination against disabled and elderly populations, violation of individual rights, and questions about who determines "quality of life." Most healthcare systems try to balance efficiency with equity and human dignity considerations.
