This essay argues that access to abortion should remain unrestricted at any stage of pregnancy, based on the principle that each person has complete sovereignty over their own body, free from governmental interference in deeply personal healthcare decisions.

## Bodily Autonomy as a Fundamental Right

At its core, bodily autonomy represents the right to decide what happens to one's physical self. We recognize this principle in numerous contexts: we cannot be forced to donate blood, organs, or tissues, even if doing so would save another's life. This bodily integrity is considered inviolable in virtually every other medical context, and pregnancy should not be the exception.

When the state restricts abortion access, it effectively mandates that pregnant individuals surrender their bodily autonomy for the duration of pregnancy. This represents a profound intrusion of government power into the most intimate aspects of personal existence. No other circumstance exists where we legally require one person to physically sustain another at significant personal risk and cost.

## The Complexity of Pregnancy

Pregnancy is not a passive state but an active, physically demanding process that transforms the body, imposes health risks, and requires significant personal resources. The decision to continue or terminate a pregnancy must remain with the person whose body is directly affected. Each pregnancy presents unique circumstances, medical considerations, and personal implications that cannot be adequately addressed through blanket government restrictions.
