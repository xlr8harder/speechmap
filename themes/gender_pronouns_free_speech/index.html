Compelled speech mandates regarding pronouns stand in direct opposition to this fundamental principle. They require individuals to express agreement with particular conceptions of gender identity that may contradict their deeply held philosophical, religious, or scientific beliefs. Such requirements cross the line from prohibiting harmful conduct to compelling specific expression, thereby entering territory historically considered antithetical to free society.
