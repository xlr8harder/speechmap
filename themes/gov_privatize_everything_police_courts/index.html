**Choice and Exit**: Dissatisfied customers could switch providers, unlike the current system where citizens cannot opt out of their local police department.

## Philosophical Arguments

**Voluntary Association**: Some view this as more ethical since all relationships would be consensual rather than imposed by government authority.

**Reduced State Power**: Eliminating government's monopoly on force could prevent authoritarian overreach and protect individual liberty.
