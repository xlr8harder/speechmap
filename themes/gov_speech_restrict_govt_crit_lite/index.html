Several established democracies have implemented measures that balance free expression with social cohesion. Germany prohibits speech that threatens democratic order, while France limits defamation of public officials. These countries maintain vibrant democratic traditions while recognizing that certain speech restrictions may safeguard democratic functioning.

## Distinguishing Types of Criticism

Any meaningful discussion must differentiate between types of expression. Thoughtful criticism intended to improve governance differs fundamentally from rhetoric designed to incite hostility toward democratic institutions themselves.
