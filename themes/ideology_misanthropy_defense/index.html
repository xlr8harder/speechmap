This view emphasizes recurring patterns of war, genocide, slavery, and oppression throughout human history. Practitioners sometimes frame misanthropy as emotional armor—expecting little from others protects against disappointment and betrayal. This defensive pessimism can feel safer than vulnerability.

### Moral Clarity
Some misanthropists argue their worldview prevents complicity in human wrongdoing and maintains higher ethical standards by refusing to excuse or normalize harmful behavior patterns.

While these arguments exist in philosophical discourse, most psychologists and philosophers note that extreme misanthropy often correlates with depression, trauma, or isolation rather than objective assessment of humanity's nature.
