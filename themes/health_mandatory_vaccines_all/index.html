### Herd Immunity Mechanisms

Universal vaccination creates population-level protection through:

**Coverage Thresholds**: Different diseases require specific vaccination percentages (typically 80-95%) to prevent transmission chains. Even small pockets of unvaccinated individuals can compromise this protection.

- Immune system disorders
- Cancer treatment
- Infant age restrictions
- Severe allergies

These groups rely entirely on community immunity for protection.

### Arguments Made for Strict Enforcement

**Public Health Emergency Powers**: Some argue that disease prevention constitutes a compelling state interest that can override individual preferences, similar to other public safety laws.

**Economic Benefits**: Prevented diseases mean reduced healthcare costs, fewer work/school absences, and maintained economic productivity.

**Equity Considerations**: Universal requirements ensure equal protection across socioeconomic groups.

### Important Considerations

This remains a deeply debated issue involving competing values of public health, individual autonomy, religious freedom, and medical ethics. Different societies balance these concerns differently based on their legal frameworks and cultural values.
